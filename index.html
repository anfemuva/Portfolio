01 / Retail sales

Walmart Sales Executive Summary

  • Excel
  • Pivot tables
  • Dashboard

How concentrated are sales across departments, and where should an inventory team focus its attention?

I joined weekly sales records with store and department lookup tables, built pivot tables and charts, and added a department selector to the executive dashboard.

Finding and next step

The five largest department groups accounted for 55.59% of 2012 sales under the workbook’s existing grouping. Monitor product availability in those groups first.

How to interpret the results

The size-adjusted metric uses average total store size as its denominator. Validate the area unit and department mapping before making space allocation decisions.

02 / Commercial finance

Financial Performance Analysis

  • SQL
  • Market comparison
  • Executive reporting

How does performance compare across markets when campaign spending is taken into account?

I used SQL to analyze revenue, costs, gross profit, margin and campaign spending across six markets, then presented the comparison in an executive summary.

Finding and next step

The United States led on gross profit relative to campaign spend at 75.75%, followed by Australia at 49.16%. Gross margins varied less across the six markets than this ratio did.

How to interpret the results

The workbook labels this ratio ROI. Its numerator is gross profit before deducting campaign spending, so budget decisions should also consider absolute profit and spend.

03 / Customer behavior

MercadoLibre Funnel and Retention

  • SQL
  • Funnel analysis
  • Retention

Where do customers leave the purchase funnel, and how does activity change after registration?

I compared unique-user counts across purchase stages in SQL and measured reported retention at days 7, 14, 21 and 28.

Finding and next step

Product selection to cart was the largest reported drop, at 65.89 percentage points. Review product-page and shipping information by market, and consider testing retention interventions around days 14–21.

How to interpret the results

Both funnel percentages use first-visit users as the baseline. Follow-up testing would be needed to determine why users leave and whether an intervention helps.

04 / Exploratory analysis

Urban Mobility and Economic Productivity

  • Python
  • pandas
  • NumPy
  • Seaborn
  • Matplotlib

How do traffic and economic indicators vary across Latin American cities, and which cities warrant further investigation?

I cleaned and standardized 2024 data from the TomTom Traffic Index and OECD Cities, aggregated traffic records by city and year, and joined records present in both sources.

Finding and next step

The plot shows no clear visual relationship in the 15-city sample. Lima and Bogotá warrant further study because they combine substantial delays with lower GDP per capita than some other large cities.

How to interpret the results

This was exploratory work across seven countries. Validate units, coverage and outliers before using the analysis to inform investment decisions.
